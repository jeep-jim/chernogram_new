# BriefCraft AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

BriefCraft AI is a Chrome extension for creating visual AI prompts and implementation briefs. It is local-first and does not use advertising, analytics or a developer-operated content server.

## Data handled by the extension

BriefCraft AI can handle content that you explicitly provide, including rich text, prompt fields, code, local files, images, canvas objects and project metadata. It can also handle text you explicitly select on the active website and screenshots you explicitly capture.

If you enable Google Drive sync, the extension receives a short-lived Google OAuth access token and transfers the current BriefCraft AI workspace directly between the extension and Google Drive over HTTPS. The extension does not request your Google profile name or email address.

## How data is used

Data is used only to provide user-facing features: editing, autosaving, copying, exporting, printing, local-folder storage and optional Google Drive synchronization. BriefCraft AI does not use content for advertising, analytics, profiling, credit decisions or training AI models.

## Storage and retention

Workspaces and settings are stored locally in Chrome until you delete them or uninstall the extension. If you choose a local project folder, files remain there until you delete them. If you enable Google Drive sync, one app-created file named `BriefCraft AI.workspace.json` remains in your Drive until you delete it. The OAuth access token is kept in session storage and is removed when you sign out or the session ends.

## Sharing and third parties

The developer does not receive or sell your content. When Google Drive sync is enabled, workspace data is shared with Google only as necessary to provide the sync feature and is governed by your Google account and Google's privacy terms. Content that you copy into an AI chat or export to another destination is handled by the destination you choose.

## Website access

BriefCraft AI does not continuously inspect browsing activity and does not request persistent access to all websites. Active website content is accessed only after an explicit action such as importing selected text or capturing the visible tab.

## Security

Google Drive requests use HTTPS. User-authored HTML/CSS/JavaScript previews run inside a sandboxed iframe without access to Chrome extension APIs or extension storage.

## Limited Use

BriefCraft AI's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide or improve the user-facing synchronization feature, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not made available for human reading except where required by law or with the user's explicit consent for support.

## Your choices

You can use BriefCraft AI without a Google account. You can sign out of Google Drive sync, delete local Chrome data by removing the extension, delete files from your chosen local folder, and delete the BriefCraft AI workspace file from Google Drive.
